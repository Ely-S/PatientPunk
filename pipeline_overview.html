PatientPunk — what it does

Scrapes firsthand patient reports from Reddit (r/covidlonghaulers) and mines them into structured, queryable evidence: drug sentiment (Track A → SQLite) and patient demographics + clinical variables (Track B → files). The two tracks share no database and no code — they're intentionally decoupled, joinable only via the SHA-256 author hash.
