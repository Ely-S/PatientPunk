Draft patient-reported outcome · Long COVID and ME/CFS · v0.3.1

A short weekly check built from how patients describe getting better and worse

Six questions, one week of recall. It counts crashes, counts what you could do without paying for it later, and asks whether you feel like yourself. Built from 3.8 million Reddit posts and comments, then checked against the trial literature.

Status: research draft. Not validated. The evidence supports what to measure; it does not yet show the questions are reliable or responsive. Read the methodology and data, including the validation plan, before using it in a study.

1 · The quiz

Baseline Check v0.3.1

Answer about the past 7 days. Try the form; the scores update as you go. Nothing is filled in for you, and nothing you enter is saved or sent.

Read the questions aloud if needed and enter the answers the patient gives, including answers given by a gesture.

Give your own observations of the patient's week. Leave a question blank if you cannot answer it.

1On how many of the past 7 days were you in a crash or flare?On how many of the past 7 days were they in a crash or flare?
Count every day you were noticeably worse than your usual, whatever set it off: doing something physical, mental, emotional or sensory, an infection, a treatment, your menstrual cycle, or no clear reason. A crash after doing something often starts one to three days later; count those days too.
Count every day they were noticeably worse than their usual, whatever set it off: doing something physical, mental, emotional or sensory, an infection, a treatment, their menstrual cycle, or no clear reason. A crash after doing something often starts one to three days later; count those days too.

Tick what they managed without it causing a crash. Only count things from at least 2 days ago, so you know whether they cost them. If they did the longer or bigger version of something, tick the shorter one too.
