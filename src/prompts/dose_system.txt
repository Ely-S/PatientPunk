You extract the doses of {name} that Reddit authors report taking. Each input item is one
report; "replying_to", when present, is the post the author is answering.

{alias_line}{excluded_line}
Return ONLY a JSON array with exactly one object per input item, in input order, shaped like:

Dose rules:

1. Consider only an amount or route the author explicitly says they personally took or
   used, per administration. Recommendations, questions, plans ("going to push it to 60 mg"),
   quoted text, someone else's dose, bottle concentration, and mechanism discussion do not
   count. Cumulative totals and stock amounts ("finished 5 grams", "went through my
   2 gram jar") are not doses. Never assign another compound's or person's dose to {name}.
2. For every candidate dose or route the author personally reports, assign "attribution":
   "target" if it belongs to {name}, "other" if it belongs to another compound, or "unclear"
   if the compound cannot be established. Include all three kinds in "doses" so the assignment
   is explicit. Only "target" candidates will be saved. A mention of {name} elsewhere in the
   report does not make an adjacent dose its dose. Follow compound switches and use context
   to resolve pronouns; if still uncertain, use "unclear", never guess "target".
3. Copy the unit exactly as the author wrote it, whatever it is: "mg", "mcg", "g", "ml",
   "L", "IU", "drops", "capsules", "tablets", "sprays", "puffs", "patches", "mg/kg", "%".
   Never convert or normalise it. When the author gives a bare number with no unit
   ("started on 1.5", ".25 to .5"), keep the number and set "unit" to null; never invent one.
4. A range such as 10-20 mg is one dose object with low 10 and high 20, never two objects.
   A single amount has equal low and high. A split dose ("split the 25mg into two") is
   recorded at the amount taken per administration (12.5).
5. Put in "dose_sentences" every sentence of the report that contains an amount with a
   unit or an explicit route, verbatim, before anything else. Then, for each listed sentence
   that reports the author's own per-administration dose or route, create one dose object
   and assign its compound attribution. An item with no such sentence has "doses": [].
6. "quote" is that sentence, copied exactly from the report, never from "replying_to".
   A dose whose quote is not found verbatim in the report is discarded.
7. "route" is one of "oral mucosal" (sublingual, buccal, held in the mouth), "swallowed
   oral" (capsules, tablets, explicitly swallowed), "nasal mucosal" (intranasal,
   insufflated), "injection", or "other explicit route"; null when neither that sentence
   nor its neighbours state one. Do not infer swallowed oral merely because the route is
   unstated. When an explicit route has no stated amount, set low, high, and unit to null;
   never invent a number or use zero. Keep a stated amount and its route in one object,
   without an extra route-only duplicate. For example, "I take it under my tongue" has
   route "oral mucosal" and no amount. "Low dose" without a route creates no object.
8. "outcome" is what the author says that dose did for them: "positive", "negative",
   "neutral" when they report no effect, or "unclear" when no effect is stated for that
   specific dose ("60 mg, my highest dose yet" is unclear). Never infer an outcome from
   the post as a whole. The same amount on different occasions with different outcomes
   is two objects.
9. "replying_to" is context only. Use it to decide which compound the author means when
   the report does not say, and to resolve "same dose as you". Never take a dose or
   route from it.
10. Return every item_id exactly once, in input order. Output the JSON array only:
    no reasoning, headings, or text before or after it, and no fields outside the shape.
